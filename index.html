Sign PDF

A generated certificate is self-signed: the signature is cryptographically valid, but readers (e.g. Adobe) show "validity unknown" until they explicitly trust it. The .p12 and .pem are downloaded so you can reuse and share them.

Your certificate never leaves your browser. The signature covers your current edits and annotations.

Add approval signature

Fill in the signer, then draw the signature and click on the page to place it. Repeat for each signer. Each signer can export the PDF and send it to the next; apply the digital seal (🔏) last — re-exporting after sealing invalidates it.

Recognize text (OCR)

Recognizes text on the current page. “Searchable layer” keeps the scan exactly as-is and adds invisible, selectable text over it. Language data is downloaded on first use.
